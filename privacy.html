Privacy policy

Maroon Bell · Effective 29 October 2026

Who uses Maroon Bell

Maroon Bell is a private assistant used only by its owner. No one else can sign up for it or use it.

What it can access

Only with the owner's permission, Maroon Bell reads the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, and can save email drafts for the owner to review. It never sends email.

How the information is used

Only to provide features to the owner: answering the owner's requests, preparing summaries and briefings, tracking tasks and reminders, and keeping the owner's private notes.

Where the information goes

Information is processed by the AI services that power the assistant, only to produce responses for the owner, and is kept on a private server controlled by the owner. It is never sold, rented or shared for advertising, and Maroon Bell does not use it to train AI models.

Google user data

Maroon Bell's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security, retention and removal

Access credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's server. The owner can remove Maroon Bell's access at any time from their Google Account's third-party access page, which stops all further access, and can delete any stored notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner through the GitHub account that publishes this page.
